Pokerole Dynamic Sheets

Character sheets and a wild-encounter builder for the Pokerole tabletop RPG. Your data stays in plain .json files on your own computer.

Trainer's License

Stats, skills, team, PC storage, bag and equipment. Start here.

 Pokémon Card

One Pokémon's sheet — a team member, a boxed one, or a wild encounter.

 GM Screen

Roster, combat tracker, dice, NPC name generator and session notes.

 World Builder

Regions, the cities in them and the rooms inside: paint terrain, place landmarks, routes and tokens, and step from one into the next.

 Rolling Table

Play together online: a shared dice feed, the GM's map and music, and the turns of a fight.

 Contact Center

Report a bug, suggest an improvement or ask for something new — and read what others posted.
