# Equipment icon pack — what fits the Pokémon world

> **Historical note.** This was the first pass, and it only covers the colour
> pack. The catalogue has moved on since: the equipment window now also draws
> from `app-data/dnd-monochrome-icons/`, another round of filtering dropped the
> things a trainer can't wear or carry (siege engines, vehicles, deeds, filled
> potion bottles), and categories were reassigned by what an item *is*.
> **`app-data/equip-icons-db.js` and `app-data/equip-icons-mono-db.js` are the
> source of truth** — the counts and groupings below are no longer accurate.

Every icon in `app-data/dnd-item-icons-by-gwill-main/` (908 total), split by
one rule: **if the thing exists in real life, it fits.** Real tools, clothing,
food, instruments, historical armour and weapons, real animals, gems, metals,
vehicles and siege engines are in. Magic, fantasy races and creatures, invented
materials and fictional games are out.

Grouped by the pack's own folders. Arguable calls sit at the end of each group.
